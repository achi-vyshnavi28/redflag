# Customer feedback: 3 conversations with people who do financial analysis

**Goal:** real input from people who do diligence or credit work, turned into product improvements.
*These conversations must be done by me and the notes must be real. Nothing below is filled in by AI.*

## Who (any 3)
- A chartered accountant in audit or transaction advisory
- A credit analyst at a bank or NBFC
- A finance student or graduate who has done an equity-research or IB internship

## Questions (20 minutes)
1. Walk me through the last time you had to understand a company from its documents. What did you open first?
2. Which part took longest? Where did you copy numbers by hand?
3. Tell me about a time a number was wrong or inconsistent between two places. How did you notice?
4. If a tool gave you an answer with a page citation, what would make you trust it? What would make you stop using it?
5. (Show RedFlag's memo on one DRHP.) What is useful here? What is missing? What would you never trust it with?
6. For loans: how do you track covenants and reporting deadlines today? What slips through?
